What do testers do?

Use Parlance like you normally would. When something looks wrong, tell us. On iPhone you can take a screenshot inside TestFlight to send feedback straight to us.

Will my journal be safe?

Yes. Your entries stay on your phone, and beta updates install over the top without erasing them.

Does it cost anything?

No. Beta builds are free. Purchases inside TestFlight are test purchases and you are never charged.
